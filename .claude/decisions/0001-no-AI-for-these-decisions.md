# No AI for these decisions

## Decisions

- Do not use AI agents to modify files in this /decisions folder.
- AI may read files in here, but cannot make changes.
- Do not use any AI to generate or edit text that you copy/paste in here.

## Context

These ADRs are our AI governance. They outline our overarching principles on how and when we use AI. We want to make sure to be precise and clear on this. When writing these ADRs, we don't want to use AI for help, because these should be solely human decisions that govern our AI usage. AI's opinion or way to phrase things has no place here.
